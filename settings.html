Appeals

- Show/hide checkbox for appeals.

Misc

- Timer starts/restarts after entering the drug name. Resets when the reset button clicked.
- Theme select is always in the top right, even if you can't see it.

- Preview updates as you go, so no guessing how your responses will affect the note.

Quantity

- Based on the drug name, Note helper will predict the quantity and day supply.
- If unchecked, quantity will be hidden unless 70 and/or 76 are checked.
